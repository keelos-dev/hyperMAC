# Third-party code in hyperMAC

## IronRDP

- Source: https://github.com/Devolutions/IronRDP, rev `8f9bf5b8102dd3c4cdb4841939d4dbba35c7bff8`
  (the same pin as keelOS's keel-rdp)
- License: MIT OR Apache-2.0 (the MIT text is in `LICENSE-IronRDP-MIT`)
- Used as a library from `vendor/IronRDP` (put there by `tools/vendor-ironrdp.sh`), with
  `ironrdp-keel.patch` applied (keelOS's patch for keel-rdp), mainly:
  - `ironrdp-acceptor` / `ironrdp-server`: CredSSP looks up the password (or its NT hash) by the
    user name the client sends; Hybrid security also accepts clients that want TLS only.
  - `ironrdp-server`: application-defined dynamic channels (`keelOS::Video`), reliable UDP
    transport (MS-RDPEUDP2) beside TCP, automatic-reconnect cookies taken as a new login,
    pointer updates that were not sent.
  - `ironrdp-egfx`, `ironrdp-dvc`, `ironrdp-rdpeudp-tokio`: fixes that keel-rdp needed.
  and then `ironrdp-hypermac.patch` (hyperMAC's own): a redraw the client asks for makes
  `ironrdp-server` send the next picture whole.

## Rust crates

Every other dependency comes from crates.io under its own license. `THIRD_PARTY-crates.txt` lists
each crate of `Cargo.lock` with its license and the license / notice texts it ships (generated by
`tools/third-party.py` with keelOS's license checker; the file names the `Cargo.lock` it is of).

## Virtual machines: bhyve, EDK2

The virtual machine programs in the app (`hypermac-vm`, `bhyve`, the UEFI firmware `QEMU_EFI.fd`)
have their notices in `THIRD_PARTY-vm.txt` (written by `tools/third-party-vm.py` when the app is
packed): FreeBSD's bhyve (BSD licenses, the notice of every source file), EDK2's ArmVirtQemuKernel
firmware (BSD-2-Clause-Patent) and the Rust crates of `vm/Cargo.lock`.

`bhyve` also contains libtpms (the TPM 2.0 of a Windows machine; BSD-3-Clause) and OpenSSL's libcrypto
(Apache-2.0), linked statically; their license texts are in `THIRD_PARTY-vm.txt` too.

Not distributed with hyperMAC, provided by the user: the virtio-win driver ISO, the systems' own
installers.

## Apple frameworks

ScreenCaptureKit, VideoToolbox, CoreGraphics, AppKit, Virtualization, Hypervisor and the other system frameworks are part
of macOS and are not distributed with hyperMAC.

`assets/os/freebsd.svg`) are from Simple Icons (https://simpleicons.org, CC0 1.0). The marks
themselves are trademarks of their owners and stand here only for the system a machine runs.
